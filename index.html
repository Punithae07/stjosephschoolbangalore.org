About Us

St. Joseph English High School is run by the sisters of the Congregation of the Immaculate Conception (CIC). The Congregation of the Immaculate Conception has a very large scope of spiritual, social, medical and educational services in India, Italy, Germany, Africa (South Africa and Zambia) and Sri Lanka.CIC had its origin in 1911.
